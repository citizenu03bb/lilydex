What is kept, and why

  • Your account, created by your first request or by signing up: display name, email address and, if you chose one, your password in hashed form (unreadable, even by the seller). It is used to handle your reservation requests: this is the legal basis of the processing (pre-contractual measures, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Your reservation requests and their message, for the same reason.
  • Your IP address, to protect the site against abuse (a limited number of sign-ups, requests and sign-ins per address): in memory only, and in the server’s technical log. Legal basis: the legitimate interest in securing the site (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Anonymous audience measurement, without cookies: each day the site counts its visitors, the pages viewed, the cards opened and the site visitors come from. To count the same device only once a day, it keeps a scrambled fingerprint of your IP address and browser, deleted that same evening; only totals remain afterwards. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control opt-out signal, you are not counted.

Nothing is sold, shared for commercial purposes or used for advertising. The site uses no trackers: only a sign-in cookie and your basket, kept in your browser.

For how long

  • Declined account: deleted 30 days after sign-up.
  • Account never approved: deleted 90 days after sign-up or the last request.
  • Request without follow-up (declined, expired or cancelled): deleted 6 months later.
  • Account with no request for 3 years: deleted, along with its requests.
  • Technical log (IP addresses): 3 months.
  • Audience measurement: fingerprints deleted at the end of the day, totals kept for 25 months.
  • Backup copies: 12 months at most, then 30 days in a bin. A deleted account is never brought back by a restore.

After a completed sale, the lot stays in the seller’s accounts; deleting your account removes your name, email address and message from it.

Where

The site is hosted by Oracle Cloud in Marseille (France). Backup copies are kept on Google Drive, encrypted before upload since 18 October 2026: Google cannot read them. Google may store them in the United States, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

Once signed in, My account lets you correct your name and email address, download all your data (JSON file) and delete your account, without asking anyone.

You may at any time request access to your data, its rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, and object to the processing of your IP address, by writing to the contact below; you will receive a reply within one month. If you believe your rights are not respected, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr, in French).

The shop can be browsed and a reservation requested without a password; without a password, you exercise your rights by writing to the contact below.
